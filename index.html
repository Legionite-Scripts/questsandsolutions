About the Firm

Counsel Built on
Conviction & Craft

Founded with a singular mandate — to deliver advisory of institutional quality to a curated circle of clients — Quests & Solutions Advisory Limited operates at the intersection of capital intelligence and strategic execution.

We are not a deposit-taking institution. We hold no banking licence and seek none. Our independence is our most valuable asset: we advise without conflict, act without agenda, and speak with the candour that only a privately-held firm can afford.

Our principal relationships span founders building globally, family offices navigating succession, and multinationals seeking grounded intelligence on the Nigerian and broader African operating environment.
